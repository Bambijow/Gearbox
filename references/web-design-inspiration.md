# Visual inspiration and Inspo evidence

External references are evidence and calibration, never a template to clone.

## When Inspo is available

Prefer a compact research sequence:

1. start from the real product brief with Inspo `recommend` or a targeted screen search;
2. pick 2-4 exemplars that illuminate different useful directions, not four near-identical category leaders;
3. use `compare` when triangulating a house style or separating category gravity from deliberate differentiation;
4. use `get_design_system` only for references whose token/type/system choices actually matter to the decision;
5. use component/example search when a specific information structure is under consideration.

Avoid exhaustive browsing. The goal is a better decision, not a mood-board dump.

## Evidence packet

Capture:

- selected screen/site identifiers;
- useful macrostructure;
- typography/material/palette disciplines;
- repeated category patterns;
- meaningful outliers;
- one or two anti-references;
- what Gearbox intends to borrow at the principle level;
- what Gearbox intentionally refuses or changes.

Category consensus is gravity, not law. A strong result may follow it or deliberately oppose it.

## Reference, do not clone

Do not reproduce another site's identity, layout, copy, imagery or signature interaction wholesale. Combine observations into an original design direction grounded in the user's product truth.

A good note says:

- "borrow dense editorial hierarchy and shallow radii";
- "use a similar information rhythm but different composition";
- "avoid the category's default violet-gradient card stack".

A bad note says:

- "make it look exactly like Linear/Stripe/etc.";
- "copy this hero and replace the logo".

Canonical/reference JSX may be studied for structure when legally and operationally appropriate, but implementation must be adapted to the target system and design contract rather than pasted as an identity shortcut.

## When Inspo is unavailable

Continue using repository truth, existing screenshots/tokens/components and first-principles design reasoning. Missing inspiration tooling should reduce external evidence, not block the lane.
